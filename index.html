Under review, Columbia Junior Science Journal

Does Dhaka's air get cleaner when the city empties?

Dhaka empties out for each Eid as people travel home. I used those holidays as a natural experiment: compare PM2.5 during Eid with the weeks around it, for every Eid from 2016 to 2024, and check the result with 2,000 placebo runs.

36%lower PM2.5 during Eid
14 of 16holidays showed a drop

Average of 16 Eid holidays, as % of each holiday's normal level. Shaded band: 95% bootstrap interval. Hover to read each day.

Sole author. 75,325 hourly readings. 95% confidence interval: 22% to 47% lower. Code and data

Under review, Air Quality, Atmosphere & Health

Forecasting tomorrow's PM2.5 in Dhaka

A model that predicts tomorrow's average PM2.5 from one reference monitor, ERA5 weather and satellite fire data. The bar to beat is "tomorrow equals today", a strong baseline for daily air quality.

5.22µg/m³ lower error than that bar
p = 0.022Diebold-Mariano test

Sole author. XGBoost, checked with walk-forward testing and an independent LightGBM model. Code

I am translating the 12,000-question MedQA medical exam into Bangla so the same questions can be asked in both languages. In a 500-question pilot of 5 open models, a revised prompt cut flagged translations for the chosen model from 399 to 100.
